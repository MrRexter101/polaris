— 07 How it works

Four steps from first call to steady progress.

A program manager oversees scheduling and session quality. A regional partner keeps parents updated throughout.

Book step 1, it's free
  1. 01

    Free consultation 30 min

    Student and parents meet a Program Director to talk through current challenges, target UK or US direction, timeline and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Pathway assessment

    We map the full picture: curriculum (IB, A-Level, IGCSE, AP, SAT/ACT), subject-by-subject performance, target pathways and learning gaps.

  3. 03

    Your Polaris plan

    A personalised tutoring plan with matched tutors, a session schedule and progress tracking, overseen by your program manager.

  4. 04

    Global mentor session

    For every 11 sessions, a 45-minute session with a programme director on university preparation, study direction and long-term goals.

  5. University. A clear, steady route there.
